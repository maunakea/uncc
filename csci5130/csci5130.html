CSCI 5130. Computer Graphics--Design of Interactive Systems.
Prerequisites: CSCI 1214 or equivalent and MATH 2164 or equivalent. Introduction to the design and implementation of interactive graphics systems. Raster and vector display systems, I/O devices; graphics primitives and their attributes; raster algorithms and clipping; 2D/3D geometric transformations; 3D viewing and projections; hierarchical and procedural models; surface representation; color and lighting models; rendering algorithms; global illumination and texture mapping.View Syllabus
